# Safari Hub — User Manual

**Kenya’s digital hospitality ecosystem**  
Version: July 2026

Safari Hub is a hospitality **operating system**: travellers book stays and experiences; operators run demand, staffing, suppliers, payments and compliance in one place.

Open the app in a browser (it also works as a Progressive Web App — install from your browser menu for home-screen access).

## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 Create an account

1. Go to **Join** (`/register`).
2. Choose:
   - **Traveller** — book stays, dining, transport and experiences.
   - **Operator (provider)** — run a hotel, venue, tour, restaurant or transfer business. Enter business name and KYC (national ID or company registration).
3. Sign in anytime via **Log in** (`/login`).
4. Forgot password? Use **Forgot** (`/forgot`) then reset from the email link.

### 1.2 Roles at a glance

| Role | Home after login | Main job |
|------|------------------|----------|
| Traveller | My trips (`/account`) | Discover, book, pay, message hosts |
| Provider | Operating hub (`/provider`) | Run the business + receive demand |
| Admin | Admin console (`/admin`) | Approve, pay out, govern the platform |

Header **Dashboard** always takes you to the right home for your role.

## 2. Travellers (tourists)

### 2.1 Discover

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Home `/` | Search by place, county and type |
| Browse `/browse` | All listings; filter by category, price, amenity, guests |
| Categories | Stays · Eat & go out · Move · Explore · Meet |
| Events `/events` | What’s on — then book a stay nearby |
| Packages `/packages` | Bundled multi-day journeys |

**Tips**

- Use **county** to narrow to live markets.
- Guests filter appears for Stays and Meet; Eat / Move / Explore focus on place and price.
- Empty results: clear filters or try another category.

### 2.2 Book a listing

1. Open a listing from Browse.
2. Review photos, amenities, location, reviews and payment options (M-Pesa, card, cash on arrival).
3. Choose offer (room, day pass, ticket, activity, etc.), dates and guests.
4. Select payment method and confirm.
5. For **M-Pesa**: enter your phone, approve the STK prompt on your handset.
6. Keep your **booking reference**. Open the VAT receipt from your trip when paid.

You can **Save** a listing (♡) and **Ask a question** (opens Messages after sign-in). WhatsApp may be available when the host publishes a phone number.

### 2.3 Your account

| Menu | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **My trips** | See bookings, cancel when allowed, leave a review after the stay |
| **Messages** | Chat with providers |
| **Saved** | Wishlist |
| **Loyalty** | Points earned from paid trips |
| **Profile** | Name, contact, password |

### 2.4 Cancel

Cancellable statuses are typically pending / reserved / confirmed (before check-in or event start). Paid amounts are marked refunded in Safari Hub; the bank or M-Pesa timeline follows the processor. See `/legal/cancellation`.

## 3. Operators (providers)

After registration, admin must **approve** your business before listings go fully live (unless auto-approve is on). Complete the **Operating checklist** on the hub.

### 3.0 Multiple businesses

One login can own **several businesses** (e.g. a lodge in Turkana and a restaurant in Nakuru).

1. Open **Businesses** (`/provider/businesses`) or the sidebar switcher.
2. **Add another business** — name + optional KYC.
3. **Switch** the active business — Listings, Bookings, Staff, Suppliers, Compliance and Payouts all apply to the active one.
4. Staff invites are per business.

Your personal **Account profile** stays shared across all businesses.

### 3.1 Operating hub (`/provider`)

Your daily home. It shows:

- Approval / KYC / live listings / payout phone / inbox / eTIMS queue
- KPIs: listings, bookings, revenue, rating
- Shortcuts into OS modules
- Active business switcher

### 3.2 Demand

| Module | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Listings** | Create and edit places, rooms/offers, photos, location, categories |
| **Inbox** | Answer guest questions |
| **Bookings** | Confirm stays, mark cash collected, manage guests |
| **Inquiries** | Legacy lead forms |
| **Reviews** | Read feedback and reply |

**Listing tips**

- Add cover photo, clear description, map pin, and at least one offer (room / tour / ticket / package).
- Submit for approval when complete; unfinished items appear on the hub checklist.
- Public storefront: `/providers/[your-slug]` (link on the hub).

### 3.3 Operations

| Module | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Staffing** | Invite team by email — roles: OWNER, MANAGER, FRONT_DESK, ACCOUNTANT. If they already have an account, they are attached immediately. |
| **Suppliers** | Order food, linen, security and services from the B2B marketplace; track order status |
| **Compliance** | KYC status; save KRA PIN; queue paid receipts for **eTIMS**; mark submitted with a KRA reference |

### 3.4 Finance

| Module | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Payouts** | Net earnings after commission; wait for admin M-Pesa B2C settlement |
| **Insights** | Revenue, bookings and ratings for your business |

Set a **payout phone** on **Business profile** before expecting M-Pesa payouts.

### 3.5 Typical operator week

1. Publish / update listings.  
2. Reply in Inbox the same day.  
3. Confirm bookings; collect cash on arrival when applicable.  
4. Queue paid receipts in Compliance.  
5. Place supplier orders as needed.  
6. Check Payouts after the settlement cycle.

## 4. Administrators

Sign in as Admin → **Dashboard** (`/admin`).

### 4.1 Approvals & trust

| Area | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Approvals → Pending** | Approve or reject new providers (KYC) |
| **Approvals → Approved** | Review already approved businesses |
| **Listings** | Moderate catalog items |
| **Reviews** | Moderate public feedback |
| **Users / Roles** | Accounts and permissions |
| **Activity logs** | Audit trail |

### 4.2 Catalog & markets

| Area | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Content** | Publish events and travel packages |
| **Markets** | Turn counties live so they appear in Browse |
| **Ads & broadcasts** | Platform announcements |

Phase rollout tip: keep only target counties (e.g. Turkana / Northern Kenya) live until you expand.

### 4.3 Bookings & finance

| Area | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Bookings** | Platform-wide booking oversight |
| **Payouts** | Pay providers via M-Pesa B2C (ensure Daraja B2C credentials and provider payout phone) |
| **Suppliers** | Confirm / fulfil / cancel B2B supplier orders |

### 4.4 Insights & settings

| Area | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Insights** | Revenue, bookings, listings, counties, trends |
| **Settings** | Branding, fees, M-Pesa, email, feature flags, eTIMS mode, legal copy |
| **Inbox** | Platform inquiries |

Configure **Settings → M-Pesa** and **Email** before go-live. See the project README launch checklist.

## 5. Payments & receipts

| Method | Who | Notes |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **M-Pesa (STK)** | Traveller | PIN on phone; wait for confirmation |
| **Card** | Traveller | Sandbox or manual confirmation per Settings |
| **Cash on arrival** | Traveller + operator | Operator marks collection in Bookings |
| **VAT receipt** | Traveller | Available for paid bookings (`/receipts/[id]`) |
| **Provider payout** | Admin → Provider | M-Pesa B2C to payout phone |

Commission is set per provider (default from Settings). Net amount appears under Provider → Payouts.
